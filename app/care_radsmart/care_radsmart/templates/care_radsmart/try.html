The whole pipeline in about ten minutes

  1. Plan a course

    Pick a patient, tick what applies and save. RAD-SMART proposes a first day and one daily time, with its reasons. Press Explain, then Approve. Tick Emergency start today to see an urgent patient fitted in today.

    Open as the doctor

  2. See it on the patient's phone

    The patient's own page in Kannada, with a voice note. Tap On my way, or ask for a morning time. Keep the six letters under the QR code for step 3.

    Open the patient's page

  3. Check the patient in

    Type the six letters from the patient's page, or scan the QR code with a camera.

    Open as reception

  4. Run the day

    Call next, In room and Out. Press First on a patient for an emergency and watch them go to the top of the queue. Accept a better time RAD-SMART offers a patient.

    Open as the technologist

  5. The machine breaks down

    Report a fault, say 90 minutes. RAD-SMART re-plans the rest of the day in seconds under the department's rules. Nothing changes until you approve.

    Report a breakdown

  6. Tell the patients

    Every patient's message, drafted in their language with a voice note. Listen, then open WhatsApp with it filled in{% if telegram %}, or send one to your own Telegram{% endif %}.

    Open the messages

  7. See the waits

    Waiting times from the times the board recorded, against the department's baseline of 109 minutes.

    Open Insights

In real use

  • Each person signs in to CARE with their own account, and RAD-SMART checks the role for every step: only a doctor approves a course.
  • Nothing changes a patient's time until a person approves it, and every change is recorded with who made it.
  • Patients get their messages on WhatsApp, with a private link to their page: no app and no login for them.
