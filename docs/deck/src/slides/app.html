6 / 10

RAD-SMART now runs inside CARE, the Open Healthcare Network's open-source hospital information system, as a backend and a frontend plugin. The doctor starts from the patient's visit page, fills in the prescription and treatment days, and ticks the priority checklist. RAD-SMART proposes the new-start slot before 17:00 and one standing slot, free on every treatment date, which the patient keeps until the course ends. The technologist records arrival, room in and out, and machine status on the day board. When the machine breaks down, RAD-SMART proposes the response under the department's rules, with messages drafted in each patient's language, and a technologist or doctor approves it. The same button-on-the-visit-page pattern applies to the hospital's own EMR.
