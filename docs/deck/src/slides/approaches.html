Score out of 5 for safety, impact, speed, ease of building, explaining and languages. A chatbot alone cannot promise the rules; a solver alone cannot learn or talk to patients. The hybrid uses each where it is strongest.

4 / 9

We scored five designs on seven weighted criteria. Fixed templates are simple but cannot absorb variation or faults. An LLM alone cannot guarantee that no new start runs past 17:00. Optimisation alone guarantees rules but cannot learn or recover live. Deep reinforcement learning is opaque and needs huge data. The hybrid predicts, optimises, simulates and explains, and it can be built in layers: the optimiser with average tables works from week one.
