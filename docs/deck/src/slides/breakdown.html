Breakdowns at 10:00 (150 min) and 11:00 (45 min), 300 simulated days, synthetic patients · rules of 24 Oct 2026

10 / 12

The department's breakdown rules of 24 October are built in. A breakdown lasts 15 minutes to 48 hours. Up to 60 minutes, nobody is sent home and everyone is treated with overtime. Longer than that, RAD-SMART sends back only as many patients as the downtime needs, those who have not left home first, and the missed session is added at the end of the course. New starts wait for the machine, and patients at home are told to postpone travel. In the 150-minute test, one patient who had not left home was sent back, two new starts moved to the next day, and everyone else was treated the same day. Compared with keeping the plan unchanged, the typical wait falls from 104 to 39 minutes. After a 45-minute breakdown it falls from 53 to 37 minutes and nobody is sent home.
