Why it is hard

Allocation under hard rules and soft priorities

Hard rules: never broken

  • New starts finish before 5 PM
  • SBRT, SRT, TBI and CSI inside the senior-staff window, 10 AM – 5 PM
  • Blood irradiation slot, 1:30 – 2 PM
  • Breast boards and ABC shared with the CT simulator
  • Versa HD and Halcyon capabilities
  • Capacity held for same-day urgent starts

Soft priorities: traded off

  • Elderly and public-transport patients earlier
  • Inpatient, hospice and dormitory time windows
  • Paying-patient preference, as the department sets it
  • Complex cases in the 12:00 – 1:30 protected block
  • As little overtime as possible

And every day brings events: machine faults, overruns, no-shows and urgent add-ons, each of which should trigger a re-plan.
