Why it is hard

Allocation under hard rules and soft priorities

Hard rules: never broken

  • New starts finish by 17:00
  • SBRT, SRT, TBI, CSI in 10:00–17:00
  • Blood irradiation 13:30–14:00, daily
  • 1 breast board, 1 ABC, shared with CT
  • MHRC: 16:00–17:00, then the 17:00 bus
  • Public transport home by 21:00
  • Urgent starts treated by 18:00

Soft priorities: traded off

  • Paying patients' preferred times
  • Older patients earlier
  • Ward, dormitory, nearby: late evening
  • Complex cases in 12:00–13:30
  • A day of 08:30 to 01:00, little overtime

And every day brings events: machine faults, overruns, no-shows, urgent add-ons and imaging holds, each of which should trigger a re-plan.

Rules confirmed by the department, 21 Sep 2026 · Assistive, not diagnostic · synthetic patients

3 / 14

As the problem statement says, this is not appointment booking. It is dynamic allocation of machine time, staff and shared resources. The department confirmed these rules on 21 September: one breast board and one ABC unit with set reservations for the linac, the 17:00 hospital bus for MHRC patients, public transport home by 21:00, and patients reporting 20 minutes early, or 45 for pelvic patients. Every rule is configuration the department can change, not code.
