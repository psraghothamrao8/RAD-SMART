Why it is hard

It is not just booking appointments

Sessions differ

A routine session takes 7–15 minutes; a new patient or a complex case takes 20–60.

Rules must hold

New patients before 17:00 · hospice patients back for the 17:00 bus · bus users home by 21:00 · one breast board, shared

The day changes

Breakdowns of 15 minutes to 48 hours, urgent patients, late arrivals and no-shows.

Each patient keeps one time slot for the whole course. A good plan must find that slot, keep every rule, and repair the day in minutes when something goes wrong.

This is not appointment booking; it is sharing out machine time. The department confirmed its rules on 21 September and 24 October: new starts finish before 17:00, complex cases between 10:00 and 17:00, the blood-irradiation slot at 13:30, hospice (MHRC) patients in the 16:00–17:00 slots to catch the 17:00 bus, public-transport patients home by 21:00, one breast board and one breath-hold unit shared with the CT room, and one standing slot for each course. Every rule is a setting the department can change, not code.
