A synthetic 87-patient day at 93% load, under the department's own rules. Complex cases stay in 10:00 to 17:00, new starts finish by 17:00, MHRC patients are treated in their 16:00 to 17:00 slots in time for the bus, public-transport patients are home by 21:00, and the late evening goes to inpatients, dormitory residents and people living nearby. The single breast board and ABC unit never clash with the CT simulator. Every placement carries a reason code, and each patient is told a reporting time.
