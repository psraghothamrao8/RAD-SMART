A day with RAD-SMART

Automation proposes; people decide

Two days ahead

Capacity forecast

  • Load from remaining fractions
  • Patients finishing each day
  • New starts per machine, by minutes

Person: HoD confirms who starts

Evening before

Plan tomorrow

  • Predict each session's minutes
  • Optimise; hard rules hold
  • Digital twin tests the plan

Person: senior RTT or oncologist approves

During the day

Run and recover

  • QR check-in; room times logged
  • Fault, delay or urgent start: re-plan by the department's rules

Person: senior RTT approves; patients told

After the day

Learn and report

  • Actual vs predicted minutes
  • Model retrained weekly
  • KPI and equity dashboard

Person: Team reviews overrides

Every override and actual timing becomes training data, so plans improve each week.
