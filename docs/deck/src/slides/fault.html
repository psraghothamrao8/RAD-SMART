The department's rules: for up to an hour of downtime, nobody is sent home, all ongoing patients are treated the same day, and a few new starts may be deferred, for approval. Beyond two hours, new starts move to the next day, except urgent palliative starts. RAD-SMART applies these rules. It freezes what has started and treats the patients already waiting first. It moves as few others as possible, never asks anyone to come earlier than they can, and messages only people with at least 45 minutes' notice. Every deferral goes to the oncologist or senior RTT for approval. The fair test is the same RAD-SMART plan with and without re-planning; today's booking in the twin, in grey, is shown for scale.
