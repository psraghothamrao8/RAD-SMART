The solution

How RAD-SMART works, and who decides

1 · Predict

Minutes, not head-counts

Expected and cautious (P80) minutes per session, starting from the department's estimate table.

PoC: 2.1 min error vs 2.6 for averages

2 · Optimise

Hard rules guaranteed

A solver builds the evening-before plan and repairs it live; hard rules cannot break.

PoC: 87 patients, all 7 rules re-checked

3 · Simulate

Tested before it is used

A twin calibrated on the department's records tests every plan and forecasts three weeks of load.

PoC: 300 days; 2 machines, peak load 154% → 98%

4 · Explain

Kannada, Tulu and more

A Sarvam AI copilot explains placements, edits rules from plain language and messages patients.

The LLM never schedules

People decide

Two days ahead the HoD confirms who starts · the evening before, a senior RTT or oncologist approves the plan · during the day a fault or urgent start triggers a re-plan in seconds, approved before anyone is messaged · overrides and actual times retrain the model weekly

Runs on a hospital server from an Excel list, a photo of the register (OCR), QR check-in and WhatsApp

5 / 8

Four AI steps, and a named person deciding at each point of the day. Machine learning predicts, the optimiser guarantees the rules, the twin tests the plan, and the language model explains it and talks to patients. The copilot calls the optimiser and simulator as tools; it never makes a scheduling decision, and it never sees names or phone numbers.
