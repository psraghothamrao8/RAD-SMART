People decide

The doctor approves each course. A technologist or doctor approves every change on the day. Nothing reaches a patient before that.

It never decides whether or how a patient is treated, only when. The language model explains; it never schedules.

Runs on a hospital server · works from an Excel list, a photo of the paper register, QR check-in and WhatsApp

Four steps. Machine learning predicts each session's minutes, starting from the department's own estimate table; in the prototype it is off by 2.1 minutes on average, against 2.6 for plain averages. An optimiser gives each course a new-start slot and one standing slot, and it cannot break a hard rule; a separate checker re-verifies every plan. A digital twin, calibrated on the department's records, tests each plan before use. A Sarvam AI copilot explains decisions and messages patients in their language. It never sees names or phone numbers, and it never makes a scheduling decision.
