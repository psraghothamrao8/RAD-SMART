The patient's side is built. Each patient gets one private link, sent once on WhatsApp from the department's own number. The page needs no app and no login, shows the first name only, and is in the patient's language with a switch to English: today's time to come, the next treatment days, the department's messages with a voice note, and on treatment days a QR code and six letters for reception. Four buttons let the patient say they are on the way, late, cannot come, or want another daily time; staff decide. After every approval RAD-SMART drafts each affected patient's message from fixed English templates. Sarvam Translate puts it into Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi or Konkani (Sarvam has no Tulu model, so Tulu speakers get Kannada, and staff can record a Tulu voice note), Sarvam Bulbul reads it aloud, and a technologist checks it and presses send in WhatsApp. A translation that drops or changes a time is never used: the patient gets the English text.
