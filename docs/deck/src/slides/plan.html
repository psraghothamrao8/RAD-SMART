The pilot target, at least 25% lower than the 109 minutes in the records, is set below the prototype's results (35% on the department's own days replayed, 40% over 12 synthetic weeks), because the twin is not perfect and the real gain depends on how much of today's waiting comes from scheduling. We also track lateness over an hour (22% today, aim 10% or less), punctuality, overtime and throughput. Stopping rule: if a hard rule is broken in practice, or balancing measures worsen for two weeks, the department reverts to manual mode. Budget about ₹2–3 lakh: a server, three tablets, a QR printer and messaging.
