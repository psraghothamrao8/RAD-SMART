Guardrails and pilot

Safe by design, then 90 days on the Versa HD

Safe by design

  • Proposal until approved. A senior RTT or oncologist approves every plan, re-plan and deferral
  • Rules re-checked. An independent checker verifies all 7 hard rules before publishing
  • Clinicians decide. No diagnoses, doses, images or notes are read
  • No identifiers to AI. Models see pseudonymous IDs; names stay on the hospital server
  • Full audit trail. Every plan, override, rule version and message is logged
  • Synthetic and anonymised only. Records are used as aggregates; DPDP-aligned

  • Median wait, overtime and throughput no worse
  • Hard rules kept; no group worse off

The pilot target is set below the prototype's result (42% lower than the busiest recorded days, 30% lower than all recorded days), because the twin under-predicts the longest waits. The baseline is already in the department's records and is refreshed in the first two weeks. Stopping rule: if a hard rule is broken in practice, or balancing measures worsen for two weeks, the department reverts to manual mode.
