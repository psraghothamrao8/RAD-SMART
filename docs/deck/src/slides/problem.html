The problem

Every weekday, 70–90 patients wait for one machine

Appointment times are given by hand, without knowing how many machine-minutes each patient needs. Too many people are told to come at once, and they wait, every day of a 5–7 week course, usually with a caregiver.

The load is rising, from 54 patients a weekday in October to 67 in December, and busy days are worse. 69% of India's 554 radiotherapy facilities have only one machine.

The department's own records show a daily pain that grows through the day: patients arriving before 10:00 wait a median of about 19 minutes, those arriving after 19:00 about 66, and evening patients now come about half an hour early to protect their place. A patient on a 25-fraction course who waits an extra hour a day loses about 25 hours, and so does the family member who comes along. Most Indian centres have one machine, so one bad day affects everyone.
