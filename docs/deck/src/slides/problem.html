The department's own records show the pain. One patient in ten waits more than 109 minutes, and 22% are treated more than an hour late. The wait builds through the day: about 19 minutes in the morning, about 66 in the evening. A patient on a 25-session course who waits an extra hour a day loses about 25 hours, and so does the family member who comes along. Most Indian centres have one machine, so one bad day affects everyone.
