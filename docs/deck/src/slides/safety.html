Guardrails

Safe by design

Proposal until approved

A senior RTT or oncologist approves every plan, re-plan and deferral; one-tap override.

Rules re-checked

An independent checker verifies every hard rule before a plan is published.

Clinicians decide

No diagnoses, doses, images or notes are read. Urgency and eligibility come from clinicians.

No identifiers to AI

Models see pseudonymous IDs; names and numbers are added on the hospital server.

Full audit trail

Every plan, override, rule version, model version and message is logged.

Works where the clinic is

Excel, paper via OCR, QR and WhatsApp; runs on the LAN with a printed fallback.

Designed to the DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025: notice, consent for messaging, minimisation, retention limits
