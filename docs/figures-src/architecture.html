RAD-SMART architecture

An operational layer between the patient list and the treatment machines. It is assistive: it never decides anything clinical.
Who uses it
RTT consoleLive queue board, day timeline, approve or override in one tap
Doctor / HoD dashboard3-week load forecast, new-start advice, KPIs
Patients & caregiversWhatsApp, SMS or voice call in their own language: live ETA, "running late" replies
Physics / adminRule Studio: machines, accessories, protected slots, priorities

↕
AI core: predict → optimise → simulate → explain
1 · Predict (machine learning)
Machine-minute forecaster Quantile gradient boosting gives P50 / P80 session length from technique, site, fraction number, imaging, mobility. Starts from average tables and learns from daily logs.
2 · Optimise (operations research)
Constraint scheduling engine Mixed-integer / constraint programming (OR-Tools CP-SAT, HiGHS). Hard rules can't be broken. It builds the day plan, re-plans live and balances machines by minutes.
3 · Simulate (digital twin)
Monte Carlo day model Stress-tests every plan against late arrivals, overruns, urgent add-ons and faults, and powers what-if questions and the 3-week capacity forecast.
4 · Explain & converse (LLM agents)
Ops copilot on Sarvam AI A tool-calling agent: plain-language rules become validated config, it answers "why was she moved?", and drafts messages in 10+ Indian languages (Bulbul voice, Saaras speech-to-text).
↕
Data & integration (light-touch first)
Excel / CSV treatment register Photo of paper register → Sarvam Vision OCR → human check QR check-in & QR tags on breast boards / ABC OIS (MOSAIQ / ARIA) read-only via HL7 v2 / FHIR — phase 2 PostgreSQL: courses, schedule versions, events, audit Runs on-premise; personal data stays in the hospital
The LLM never makes a scheduling decision. It calls the optimiser and simulator as tools and explains their output. Urgency, technique and machine eligibility are always entered by clinicians and physicists.
