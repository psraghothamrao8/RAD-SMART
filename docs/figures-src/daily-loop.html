How RAD-SMART runs a department, day by day

Automation proposes; people approve. Every step works with information the department already has.
Two days ahead

Capacity forecast

  • Project machine-minutes from remaining fractions
  • Count patients completing each day
  • Recommend new starts per machine, balanced by minutes
  • Oncologist / HoD confirms start dates
→
Evening before

Plan tomorrow

  • Predict each session's minutes (P50 / P80)
  • Optimise times: hard rules can't be broken
  • Digital twin checks the plan's robustness
  • RTT lead reviews, edits, approves
  • Patients get their time in their language
→
During the day

Run and recover

  • QR check-in; room entry and exit logged
  • Delay, fault, no-show or urgent patient triggers a re-plan in seconds
  • Changes kept small; deadlines protected
  • RTT approves; affected patients messaged
→
After the day

Learn and report

  • Actual vs predicted minutes stored
  • Duration model retrained weekly
  • KPIs: waits, punctuality, overtime, conflicts, equity by patient group
  • Department reviews overrides and reasons
↺ Every override and every actual timing becomes training data, so the plan gets more accurate each week without anyone writing new rules.
