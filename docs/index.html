RAD-SMART prototype results

RAD-SMART plans radiotherapy sessions by predicted machine-minutes, keeps every hard rule, and re-plans when the day goes wrong. These results come from our working prototype, run on a synthetic 67-patient day and simulated 300 times.

Recovery

When the machine fails for 30 minutes

RAD-SMART freezes what has started, re-plans the rest with the fewest changes, never asks anyone to arrive earlier than they can, and messages only patients with at least 45 minutes' notice.

Two machines, three weeks

New starts balanced by minutes, not head-count

Splitting new patients equally by head-count overloads the slower Versa HD while the Halcyon sits under-used. Recommending starts two working days ahead, by predicted minutes, fills both machines to target.

Machine learning

Predicting machine-minutes

Trained on 12,000 synthetic past sessions, a quantile gradient-boosting model learns first-fraction time, imaging type, wheelchair and stretcher transfers, and Halcyon's faster delivery, which the average table misses.

About

What this prototype is, and is not

How it works

  • Predict: quantile gradient boosting gives typical (P50) and cautious (P80) minutes per session.
  • Optimise: a two-stage mixed-integer model (HiGHS) places each session on a 2-minute grid; hard rules are constraints, preferences are costs.
  • Simulate: a Monte Carlo twin replays 300 days of late arrivals, overruns, no-shows, urgent add-ons and faults.
  • Explain: every placement carries reason codes; in the full system a Sarvam AI copilot explains them and messages patients in their language.

Assumptions and limits

    Safety by design

    • Plans and re-plans are proposals until a radiation therapy technologist approves them.
    • No diagnoses, doses, images or notes are used. Urgency and eligibility come from clinicians.
    • Language models never make a scheduling decision and never see names or phone numbers.
    • Every plan, override, rule version and message is logged.

    Reproduce it

    Python 3.10+, open-source libraries only, synthetic data generated on the fly; a full run takes about a minute or two.

    cd poc
pip install -r requirements.txt
python run_poc.py
